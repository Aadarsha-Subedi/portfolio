## Scope

Whole site, one page: the career wall. Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads from the first viewport). Audience: recruiters arriving from the resume. Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Aadarsha's career set as an International Typographic Style poster: a strict 12-column grid, one grotesque, one committed red. Refuses the dark, glowing, card-grid developer portfolio and the cream editorial look.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white paper ground, near-black ink, signal red owning whole blocks (never a sprinkled accent). One grotesque with a width axis: condensed and very large for the name and years, normal width for reading; tabular numerals. Hairline 1px rules on the grid lines, square corners, no shadows, no gradients, no glass.

STORY: In one screen the recruiter sees who this is, the current role, real work, and how to act (resume, email). Scrolling confirms every resume line with evidence: projects with screenshots and live links, experience with numbers, education, skills, contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Name in condensed display across columns 1 to 8, flush left at the top. Columns 9 to 12: a timetable of roles, years as big tabular numerals. A red block spanning columns 1 to 6 below the name holds the one-line summary and the two primary actions (Download resume, Email). Columns 7 to 12 beside it: a large ResolveAI screenshot set into the grid with its caption. Thin top bar: name mark, section links, Cmd/Ctrl+K index.

FORM: Swiss grid, position 3 on the ordered list of seven. seed:51a0befb3fa9e04e

Signature interaction: a project expands along the grid lines into its case study (shared-element layout transition) while the red marker slides to the active row; Cmd/Ctrl+K opens an index of every role and project. Motion grammar: precise slides and red clip-path wipes along grid lines, 300 to 500 ms, expo ease-out, no blur, no bounce; reduced motion keeps opacity and colour changes only.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
